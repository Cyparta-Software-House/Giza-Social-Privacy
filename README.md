# GZA Social – Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: November 9, 2026**

GZA Social ("we", "our", or "us") is a social networking application that allows users to create and share posts, upload photos, like content, share posts, and communicate with other users through private chat.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the GZA Social application.

### 1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the application, we may collect the following types of information:

**Account Information**
- Name and username.
- Email address.
- Profile information.
- Login credentials, stored securely.

**User-Generated Content**
- Text and posts published by users.
- Photos uploaded to posts.
- Likes and interactions with other users' content.

**Chat Information**
- Messages exchanged between users through private chat.
- Information associated with conversations, such as message timestamps and participants.

**Technical Information**
- Device information.
- Application usage and performance data.
- Technical logs used for security and troubleshooting.

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:
- Create and manage user accounts.
- Allow users to publish posts and upload photos.
- Enable likes and sharing features.
- Provide private messaging between users.
- Improve application functionality and user experience.
- Maintain platform security and prevent misuse.
- Provide technical support.

### 3. Posts and Uploaded Photos

Users can publish text posts and upload photos through GZA Social.

Content shared through public posts may be visible to other users of the platform.

Users are responsible for the content they upload and share. We encourage users not to publish sensitive personal information or content that violates the rights of others.

### 4. Private Messaging

GZA Social provides a private chat feature that allows users to communicate directly with one another.

Messages are processed and stored as necessary to provide the messaging service.

Users should avoid sharing sensitive personal or financial information through chat.

### 5. Information Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be shared with trusted service providers when necessary to operate the application, provide hosting, store content, or maintain technical services.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable laws or to protect users' rights and safety.

### 6. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information, posts, photos, and messages against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

### 7. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill applicable legal obligations.

Users may request deletion of their accounts and associated personal information.

When an account deletion request is processed, associated personal information and user content will be deleted or anonymized as appropriate, subject to applicable legal requirements and legitimate retention needs.

### 8. Children's Privacy

GZA Social is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to address the situation.

### 9. Third-Party Services

GZA Social may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, storage, and application functionality.

These services may process certain information as necessary to provide their services and are subject to their respective privacy policies.

### 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our application, services, or legal requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised Last Updated date.

### 11. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact us: support@cyparta.com
